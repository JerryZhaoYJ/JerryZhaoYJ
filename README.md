## Featured Projects

### [Meme Sommelier — Multimodal Humor Intelligence](https://github.com/Meme-Sommelier/meme-sommelier-agent) · [Live Demo](https://meme-sommelier-agent.streamlit.app)

Led a four-person project on 6,992 Memotion 7K memes. I built the Gemini/VLM H-layer and 3,183-row extraction pipeline, ran its supervised and unsupervised diagnostics, and created the Streamlit workbench for humor mechanisms, audience fit, landing risk, and revision guidance. The H-layer did not materially improve four-class macro-F1; it turned a noisy prediction task into an interpretable product.

### [POI Resolution Research Workflow](https://github.com/JerryZhaoYJ/poi-resolution-research-workflow)
*Public-safe synthetic case study · Evidence traceability, geospatial QA & human-review routing*

Built an independent synthetic portfolio reconstruction of a reviewer-facing place-data workflow. The runnable demo ranks fictional categories with TF-IDF, checks coordinate differences with the Haversine formula, preserves evidence-source IDs, routes weak or incomplete cases to human review, and exports traceable JSON/CSV packages. Private capstone and sponsor materials remain excluded.

### [AI Impact News Intelligence](https://github.com/JerryZhaoYJ/ai-impact-news-intelligence)

Built an audit-first NLP pipeline over 200,469 web-crawled AI/ML pages, combining crawl-aware cleaning, TF-IDF relevance filtering, curated NMF topics, lexicon-based entity context, and target-specific sentiment. The project produced 13 analytical themes and 209,456 context units; held-out relevance and sentiment macro-F1 were 0.866 and 0.682 against DeepSeek-assisted labels—not a human-gold benchmark. Company-level results are withheld pending a corrected mapping rerun.
